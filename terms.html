1. The service

Ringback provides an AI phone assistant ("the assistant") that answers calls forwarded from your phone number, sorts them into emergencies, bookings and callbacks according to the details you give us, and sends you summaries, alerts and recordings. Features depend on your plan, as described on our pricing page.

By creating an account or using the service, you agree to these terms on behalf of your business. If you don't agree, please don't use the service.

2. Your account

You log in with a one-time code sent to your email. Keep access to that email secure; anyone who can read it can log in to your account. Tell us straight away if you think someone else has accessed it.

The details you enter in the portal, such as service areas, hours, fees and emergency rules, are what the assistant uses. Keep them accurate and up to date.

3. Free trial week

New clients can use the service free for 7 days on their own number. At the end of the trial we'll ask whether you want to continue. If you don't, we switch it off and there's nothing to pay.

4. Plans and fees

  • Monthly fees and once-off setup fees are as shown on the pricing page when you sign up. Prices exclude VAT.
  • Fees are paid monthly in advance by EFT. Invoices are available in your client portal, and payment is due by the date shown on each invoice.
  • Each plan includes a number of call minutes per month. Extra minutes are charged at R5 per minute. We'll warn you before you reach your limit.
  • The founding partner plan is R1,500 per month for 60 days with no setup fee, for a limited number of businesses.
  • Calls forwarded from your phone are billed by your network at your normal rates. Those costs are yours.
  • We'll give you at least 30 days' notice of any price change.

5. Term and cancelling

After your trial, plans have a 3-month minimum term, then continue month-to-month. Either of us can cancel with 30 days' written notice, including by WhatsApp or email. The founding partner plan can be stopped at the end of its 60 days.

We may suspend the service if fees are more than 14 days overdue, after giving you notice, or immediately if the service is being misused.

6. Limits of an AI assistant

The assistant is software. It is designed to treat a call as urgent when it isn't sure, but it can mishear, misunderstand or make mistakes, and calls can fail for reasons outside our control, such as network outages.

  • It is not an emergency service. It does not dispatch help or replace 10111, 10177 or 112, and it tells callers to contact emergency services if anyone is in danger.
  • It only gives basic, widely known safety steps. It does not give repair or technical advice.
  • You remain responsible for responding to your customers, for your work, and for quotes and prices.
  • Always check important details, such as addresses and booking times, before acting on them.

  • Set up and keep call forwarding active on your phone, with our help.
  • Use the service lawfully, including the laws on call recording and personal information.
  • Respond to emergency alerts and callback requests in the time you've told us to promise callers.
