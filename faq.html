Getting started

What is Ringback?

An AI receptionist for home-service businesses. When you can't answer, it picks up in your business name, sorts out what the caller needs, books the job or flags the emergency, and sends you the details within a minute.

Which trades do you work with?

Plumbing, electrical, gates and alarms, solar and backup power, pest control, aircon and locksmiths so far. If your customers phone you when something breaks, ask us about your trade.

How long does setup take?

About 5 minutes for you to fill in your details in the portal, then a 10-minute session with us to set up call forwarding on your phone. Most businesses are live the same or next day.

Do I need a new number or phone?

No. You keep your own cellphone number. We set up call forwarding so only the calls you miss go to Ringback, and you can switch it off any time.

How does the free trial week work?

Create an account, fill in your business details and request your trial in the portal. We put Ringback on your number for 7 days at no cost. No card needed to start.

Calls and emergencies

Will my customers know it's not a person?

It introduces itself as your assistant, and if anyone asks, it says it's an AI. Callers mostly want help fast, and it beats voicemail or a busy tone.

What if it gets an emergency wrong?

When it isn't sure, it treats the call as an emergency and alerts you. You get every call as a recording, and we review calls with you weekly for the first month.

What safety advice does it give?

Only basic, widely known steps for your trade, such as switching off the geyser at the DB board or not touching a burning-smelling board. It never gives repair advice, and it tells people to call 10177 or 112 if anyone is in danger.

Does it quote prices?

Only your call-out fee, if you want it to. For anything else it tells the customer you'll quote once you've seen the job.

What happens when I'm already on a call?

The second caller is forwarded to Ringback instead of hearing a busy tone, and you get the summary as soon as they hang up.

Does it speak Afrikaans or other languages?

English on every plan, and Afrikaans on Pro. Other South African languages are in testing; ask us before relying on them.

Can I listen to the calls?

Yes. Every call has a recording link in your SMS summary and in your portal. Recordings are deleted after 90 days.

Pricing and contracts

How much does it cost?

Starter is R2,500 a month plus R3,500 setup. Pro is R4,500 a month plus R5,500 setup. Our first three businesses can join the founding partner plan at R1,500 a month for 60 days with no setup fee. Prices exclude VAT. See all plans.

What if I go over my minutes?

Extra minutes are R5 each. We'll warn you before you get close, and help you move plans if that works out cheaper.

Is there a contract?

A 3-month minimum after your trial, then month-to-month with 30 days' notice. The founding partner plan can stop at day 60.

How do I pay?

By EFT, monthly in advance. Your invoices appear in your portal under Billing, with our banking details. Use the invoice number as your reference, then tap "I've paid" and add your proof of payment if you like. We confirm it within one working day.

Does call forwarding cost me extra?

Forwarded calls are billed by your network at your normal rates, the same as forwarding to any other number.

Privacy and POPIA

Is this POPIA compliant?

We built it with POPIA in mind: callers hear a recording notice, recordings are deleted after 90 days, access is limited to you and us, and our written agreement covers how we process your customers' information for you. Read our privacy policy.

Who can see my customers' details?

Only you and Ringback. Your portal is protected by a login code sent to your email, and our database only lets each account see its own records.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. Request deletion from the Account page in your portal, or WhatsApp us. We'll confirm once it's done.

Your account

How do I log in?

Enter your email on the log-in page and we'll email you a 6-digit code. There's no password to remember.

I didn't get my code.

Check your spam folder, wait a minute, then tap "resend code". Codes expire after 10 minutes. You can also tap the sign-in link in the same email. Still stuck? WhatsApp us.

Can I change my business details later?

Yes. Update them in the portal any time, and we'll update your assistant to match.

Where do I find my invoices?

Log in and open Billing. You can view, print or save each invoice as a PDF, see what's due and what's paid, and let us know when you've paid.
