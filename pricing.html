Pricing questions

The money bits, plainly.

More answers on the FAQ page.

How does the free trial week work?

Create an account, fill in your business details and request your trial in the portal. We set Ringback up on your number for 7 days at no cost. If you don't want to continue, we switch it off. No card needed to start.

What happens if I go over my minutes?

Extra minutes are R5 each. We'll warn you before you get close, and help you move plans if that works out cheaper.

Is there a contract?

A 3-month minimum after your trial, then month-to-month with 30 days' notice. The founding partner plan can stop at day 60.

Do the call costs from forwarding cost me extra?

Forwarded calls are billed by your cellphone network at your normal rates, the same as forwarding to any other number. Check your contract or bundle.

How do I pay?

By EFT, monthly in advance. Your invoices appear in your client portal under Billing, with our banking details. Use the invoice number as your reference, then tap "I've paid" so we can match it.
