FAQ

Questions plumbers ask us.

Will my customers know it's not a person?

It introduces itself as your assistant, and if anyone asks, it says it's an AI. Callers mostly want help fast, and it beats voicemail or a busy tone.

What if it gets an emergency wrong?

When it isn't sure, it treats the call as an emergency and alerts you. You also get every call as a recording, and we review them with you weekly in the first month.

Does it quote prices?

Only your call-out fee, if you want it to. For anything else it tells the customer you'll quote once you've seen the job.

Do I need a new number or phone?

No. We set up call forwarding on your existing cellphone in about 10 minutes, and you can switch it off any time.

What about POPIA and my customers' details?

Callers are told the call is recorded. Recordings are kept for 90 days, then deleted, and only you and we can access them. It's all in our written agreement.

Does it speak other languages?

English and Afrikaans today, on the Pro plan. Other South African languages are in testing; ask us before relying on them.
