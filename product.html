The call flow

What happens when your phone rings out

01

A customer calls you

On your normal cellphone number. You answer if you can.

02

It forwards after ~20s

Unanswered, busy or unreachable calls go to Ringback.

03

Ringback answers

In your business name. It says the call is recorded and asks what's wrong.

04

It works out what kind of call it is

When it isn't sure, it treats the call as urgent.

Emergency

Safety steps and an urgent alert

Basic safety steps for your trade, then an urgent alert to you straight away.

Routine

Books a slot

Offers an open slot from your calendar and confirms it by SMS.

Quote

Takes a callback

Takes the details and tells the caller when you'll phone back.

05

You get the details

An SMS with name, number, suburb, problem and a recording. It's logged in your portal.

Features

Everything a good receptionist does, at 2 a.m. on a Sunday.

01Answers

  • Emergency triageSet up with your trade's emergencies. Unsure calls are treated as urgent.
  • Safety stepsBasic, widely known steps, like switching the geyser off at the DB board.
  • English and AfrikaansA calm, local tone. Afrikaans on the Pro plan.
  • Spam filteringSales pitches and wrong numbers get a polite message, not you.

02Acts

  • Calendar bookingOffers real open slots from your Google Calendar, with an SMS confirmation.
  • Instant SMS alertsEvery call reaches you within a minute, with a summary and recording link.
  • Follow-ups and reviewsCallers who didn't book get a follow-up. Google review requests on Pro.

03Keeps you in control

  • Client portalLog in with an emailed code to update details, pay invoices and see every call.
  • Privacy built inA recording notice on every call. Recordings deleted after 90 days.

Call forwarding

Switching it on is one code on your phone.

Pick your network and when calls should forward, then dial the code. You keep your number, the calls you answer don't change, and the same kind of code switches it off. We'll do it with you at setup if you'd rather.
