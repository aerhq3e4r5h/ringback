The call flow

What happens when your phone rings out

01

A customer calls you

On your normal cellphone number. You answer if you can.

02

It forwards after ~20s

Unanswered, busy or unreachable calls go to Ringback.

03

Ringback answers

In your business name. It says the call is recorded and asks what's wrong.

04

It acts

Emergency: safety steps and an urgent alert. Routine: books a slot. Quote: takes a callback.

05

You get the details

An SMS with name, number, suburb, problem and a recording. It's logged in your portal.

Features

Everything a good receptionist does, at 2 a.m. on a Sunday.

Emergency triage

Set up with your trade's emergencies. Unsure calls are treated as urgent, never ignored.

Safety steps

Basic, widely known steps for your trade, like switching off the geyser at the DB board, while the caller waits for you.

Calendar booking

Offers real open slots from your Google Calendar and sends the customer an SMS confirmation.

Instant SMS alerts

Every call reaches your phone within a minute of the caller hanging up, with a summary and recording link.

Spam filtering

Sales pitches, SEO calls and wrong numbers get a polite message and never interrupt you.

English and Afrikaans

A calm, local tone that says Boksburg and Bryanston properly. Afrikaans on the Pro plan.

Follow-ups and reviews

Callers who didn't book get a follow-up. Happy customers get a Google review request (Pro).

Client portal

Log in with an emailed code to update your details, manage your plan and see every call.

Privacy built in

Callers hear a recording notice. Recordings are deleted after 90 days. Only you and we can access them.
