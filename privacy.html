1. Who we are

Ringback ("we", "us") provides AI phone assistants for home-service businesses in Gauteng, South Africa. For information about our own website visitors, enquiries and client accounts, we are the responsible party under POPIA.

When we answer calls for a client business, that business is the responsible party for its callers' information, and we process it on the business's behalf as its operator, under a written agreement.

Contact us, including our Information Officer, by WhatsApp or phone on 065 234 3106, or through our contact page.

2. What we collect

Website visitors

Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Like any website, our hosting and font providers receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you load a page.

Enquiries

If you use our contact form, we collect your name, business name, trade, area, cellphone number, email address and message.

Website assistant

If you chat to or talk with the assistant on our website, we collect the conversation (text, and audio and transcripts for voice) and any details you give it, such as your name and number.

Client accounts

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, business name, trade and cellphone number, and the business details you add in the portal, such as services, service area, hours and fees.

Our clients' callers

When the assistant answers a call for a client, it collects what's needed to help: the caller's name, phone number, suburb or address, the problem, and a recording and transcript of the call.

3. How we use it

  • To answer and handle calls for our clients, and send them summaries and alerts.
  • To create and run client accounts, including sending one-time login codes by email.
  • To reply to enquiries and set up trials.
  • To check and improve how the assistant handles calls for each client, for example in weekly reviews.
  • To invoice clients and meet our legal and tax obligations.
  • To keep our services secure and prevent misuse.

We rely on the lawful grounds in POPIA section 11: performing a contract, our legitimate interests, legal obligations, and consent where we ask for it. We only send marketing messages if you've agreed to them, and you can opt out at any time.

4. Call recordings

Every call the assistant answers starts with a notice that the call is recorded. Recordings and transcripts are used to give the client an accurate record of the call and to improve how the assistant answers.

Recordings are deleted after 90 days. Only the client business and Ringback can access them. Call summaries (without audio) stay in the client's portal while their account is active.

6. Storage outside South Africa

Some of our providers store or process information outside South Africa, including in the European Union and the United States. Under POPIA section 72, we only use providers that are bound by data protection laws or agreements that give protection substantially similar to POPIA.

8. Security

Information is encrypted in transit. Client accounts are protected by one-time codes sent by email. Our database only lets each account see its own records, and access on our side is limited to the people who need it. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

9. Browser storage

We don't use tracking cookies. We use your browser's local storage only to keep you logged in to the portal and to remember that you accepted the assistant's recording notice. Logging out clears your login.

10. Your rights

Under POPIA you can:

  • ask whether we hold information about you, and for a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to us using it, including for direct marketing;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it.

Clients can download their data and request account deletion from the Account page in the portal. Anyone else can contact us on 065 234 3106. If you called one of our clients, contact that business first, as they're responsible for your information; we'll help them respond.
