About Ringback

Built in Gauteng for the trades that keep it running.

Plumbers, electricians and installers are some of the busiest people in the province, and the worst placed to answer a phone. Ringback exists so that being good at your job never costs you the next one.

Why we started

The phone rings at the worst possible moment.

You're in a ceiling, under a sink or on a ladder. The phone rings out. The customer doesn't leave a message; they call the next business on Google. Multiply that by a few calls a week and it adds up to real money.

Big companies solve this with call centres. Small, owner-run businesses can't justify a full-time receptionist for the calls that come in at 21:00 on a Sunday. AI voice assistants have finally become good enough, and affordable enough, to close that gap.

Ringback sets them up properly for local trades: South African place names, local safety steps, rand pricing, POPIA, and someone in Gauteng you can WhatsApp when you need to change something.

How we work

What you can hold us to.

Honest by default

The assistant says it's an AI if asked. We don't invent results, reviews or client numbers, and we'll tell you if Ringback isn't a good fit for your business.

Safety first

Emergencies always reach you. When the assistant is unsure, it treats a call as urgent. It gives basic safety steps, never repair advice.

Your customers stay yours

We answer in your name and send every lead to you. We never contact your customers for our own purposes or share their details.

Simple pricing

A fixed monthly price in rand, a free trial week, and a short minimum term. No hidden fees.

Founding partners

Help us build it, pay less.

We're taking on three founding partner businesses at R1,500 a month for 60 days, no setup fee. In return we ask for honest feedback, a short review and two referrals if you're happy.
